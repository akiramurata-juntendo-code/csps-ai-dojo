FICTIONAL SCENARIOS - TRAINING ONLY
Read one aloud to your phone in your own messy words, then ask:
"Turn this into a task list with owner and deadline columns."

Scenario 1 - The site visit
Thursday, six visitors from the ministry, need the main meeting room,
projector and hotspot, halal lunch for eight, transport from the airport
at nine, and someone must print the briefing pack by Wednesday.

Scenario 2 - The workshop
Next month's data-literacy workshop: 25 seats, need the training room
for two days, coffee breaks, certificates printed, invitation email to
all divisions by Friday, and a reminder one week before.

Scenario 3 - The audit file
Auditors arrive Monday. Need last year's claims folder, the asset
register updated, missing receipts chased from two officers, and a
summary sheet for the director by Friday afternoon.
