ABSTRACT
This study examines how small public organisations adopt generative AI.
Using a pre-post design with 24 participants in a single training
programme, we find a moderate improvement in self-reported confidence and
no change in reported organizational barriers. The findings suggests that
individual training is necessary but not sufficient.

3. RESULTS
Of the n = 26 participants who completed both waves, 12 (52%) reported
using the tools weekly by the second wave, compared with 5 before the
programme. Confidence rose by 0.58 points on a five-point scale
(Lim, 2023). Barrier scores were unchanged, which contradicts the
prediction set out in Section 2.

4. DISCUSSION
As Rahman and Chua (2022) argue, adoption in small administrations is
constrained less by skill than by permission. Our data is consistent with
this reading. Future work should follow participants for a full year.
