FICTIONAL INTERVIEW EXCERPTS - TRAINING ONLY
Six graduates, six months after finishing. Companion to
bn_graduate_tracer_MOCK.csv. Names, places and quotes are invented.

--- R1, Bachelor, Business, Brunei-Muara ---
"I was not unemployed exactly. I was waiting. Everybody knows the intake
announcement comes at a certain time of year, so you apply and then there
is nothing to do but wait. If I take a private job in between, I might not
be free when the interview finally comes. So you wait, and the months on
the form say you did nothing."

--- R2, Diploma, Social Sciences, Tutong ---
"I applied for eleven positions. Nine never replied at all. I did not know
whether my application was received. My cousin told me to ask an uncle who
works there - that is how it is done, he said. I did not want to do that."

--- R3, Bachelor, Engineering, Brunei-Muara ---
"The internship was the whole thing. Not the skills - I learned more in the
first month of work than in the internship. It was that someone in the
company knew my name. When the post opened, they remembered me."

--- R4, Bachelor, Education, Belait ---
"My parents were very clear. A government post is secure, it has the
pension, and it is respectable. My mother said a private company can close
tomorrow. I did apply to two private schools, but I did not tell them."

--- R5, Master, IT, Brunei-Muara ---
"I had three offers within a month. But two were in Brunei-Muara and I am
from Belait. The salary difference does not cover living away from home, so
I took the third one. People look at the months and think I was choosing
freely."

--- R6, Diploma, Islamic Studies, Temburong ---
"There is no transport problem if you have a car. I did not. Two interviews
I could not attend. Nobody asks about that on the form."

--- Note for the exercise ---
Several explanations here appear in NONE of the columns of the dataset -
the intake announcement cycle, employers who never reply, who knows your
name, and whether you own a car. Find them before you write any finding,
because a column that does not exist cannot be controlled for.
