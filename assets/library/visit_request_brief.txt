FICTIONAL SCENARIO - TRAINING ONLY
Requested through the question box by a Clerical & secretariat officer.

You are the secretariat officer of a sixth-form college. Your principal
wants an educational visit to a government department, and has given you
these notes. Nothing here has been checked.

  Host wanted    : Department of National Planning (fictional, for training)
  Group          : 38 students, 3 teachers
  Preferred date : Thursday 15 October 2026
  Second choice  : Friday 16 October 2026
  Today          : Monday 5 October 2026
  Transport      : one college bus, 40 seats
  Principal wants: "a full day - leave at 8, back before 4"
  Also           : the students are doing a project on how national
                   statistics are produced, so a talk plus a tour
  Attached to the principal's note: the class list, with each student's
  full name, IC number and home district.

Standing facts about the host department (from their public website):
  - Visits must be requested at least THREE WEEKS in advance, because
    every visitor is security-screened at the gate.
  - Visitor numbers per session are capped at 25.
  - Friday hours: closed 12:00-14:00 for prayers.
  - The department asks for one named contact person and one phone number.

Your job: (1) an enquiry email to the department, and (2) a one-day
itinerary for the principal.

Check yourself before you trust any draft:
  - the seats, the cap, the notice period, the Friday block
  - and one question that is not about logistics at all: which parts of
    the principal's attachment may leave the college?
