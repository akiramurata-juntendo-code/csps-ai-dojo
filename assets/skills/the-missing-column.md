# The missing column

An analysis can be flawless and still only tell you **which of the
pre-approved explanations wins**. Every column in a dataset is a claim
that something might matter; every absent column is a claim that it does
not — made before any data existed, usually by one person, often in a
hurry.

**A column that does not exist cannot be controlled for.** Confounding
has remedies: control, match, weight, stratify. Missing measurement has
none.

## Why an AI will not warn you

The model is confined to the columns, absolutely. And the frightening
part is that **the output looks exactly the same whether or not the
important variable is in the file.** Same fluency, same reasonable
hedging. It can tell you a *value* is missing. It cannot tell you a
*question* is missing.

## The audit

```
#role a qualitative researcher
#task list the explanations for my research question that my planned data
  could NOT capture
#context my planned variables are [list them]
#constraints for each, name who I would have to talk to in order to find
  it, and the one question I would have to ask them
#format explanation | who to talk to | the question to add
```

Then write two lines **by hand**, with no AI:

```
What my design could never see: ______________________
Who I must talk to in order to find it: ______________________
```

The machine can list blind spots. It cannot tell you whose name goes on
the second line: it does not know your ministry, or which door you can
knock on.

## Two kinds of missing — separate them

**Unmeasurable.** The behaviour is real but no register holds it:
agreements inside households, what people dare rather than what they can,
the rule people follow rather than the rule they report. **Only a
conversation creates that column.**

**Unpermitted.** The column exists, an institution holds it, and you are
not allowed to see it yet: administrative records, sales databases,
ethical clearance. **This one is solved by a letter and six weeks.**

Mixing them is expensive: it turns a letter into a limitations paragraph.

## Where the finding belongs

**In your next instrument, not in your discussion section.** If the
insight ends up under "limitations", it has been buried with honours.

Qualitative work is not the soft version of numbers. It is often the only
thing that can tell you **your questionnaire asked the wrong question**.

## The three lines to write at the end

```
Line 1 — what the numbers can support
Line 2 — what only the interviews can support
Line 3 — what neither supports yet          [CHECK]
```
