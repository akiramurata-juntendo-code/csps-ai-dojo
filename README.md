# CSPS AI Dojo — Two Schools, One Village

Companion platform for the **AI Training Workshop** at the Centre for
Strategic and Policy Studies (CSPS), Brunei Darussalam — 24–28 August 2026.
Facilitated by Dr. Akira Murata (Juntendo University).

**Live page:** https://akiramurata-juntendo-code.github.io/csps-ai-dojo/

One page carries the whole workshop journey: the pre-workshop survey, morning
review challenges, the four Hidden Scrolls (one per training day), daily recap
audio, and two spaced follow-up trials after the workshop. Content unlocks
automatically by date (Brunei time).

This repository contains no personal data — only public links and teaching
materials. Survey responses are collected and stored separately under the
study's consent and data-protection procedures.
